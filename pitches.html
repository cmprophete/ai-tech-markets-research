Start with a destination such as a shorter week or accessible expertise, then work backward. Identify the technical contribution, public investment, and institutional changes required. Say which improvements remain worth pursuing if AI disappoints.

Show the same useful capability under different contracts, bargaining arrangements, or ownership models. Keep illustrative assumptions visible and distinguish them from measured evidence. Compare credible alternatives, including their costs, rather than a good scenario against a caricature.

The path from productivity to wages, time, prices, profits, and revenueA distributional account of technological progressEconomically distinctive; connects work to householdsHard measurement; could crowd out safety and civil rightsCombine distributional economics, antimonopoly, and shared-wealth policy

Follow a saved hour or lower production cost through the economy. Ask whether it becomes more output, lower prices, higher pay, leisure, or rents, and what determines the split. Use an explicit accounting framework without pretending accounting alone identifies causal effects.

Institutions society needs to build and maintainA practical account of what government and communities must be able to doMoves beyond prohibitions; gives public options substanceCan become an institution-building catalog; execution is demandingLink public options and investment to accountable delivery

Organize around the ability to evaluate, purchase, build, enforce, and provide. Start with a public mission and identify the missing capacity, including non-AI alternatives. Explain the funding, workforce, governance, and maintenance required for each institution.

Reciprocal commitments around deployment and investmentTerms on which change earns public legitimacyCoalition-oriented; connects benefits to obligationsCan imply rights or catastrophic safety are negotiableLink public support to worker power, community benefits, and shared returns

Ask what firms owe in exchange for public investment, access, or permission to deploy in a particular setting. Separate non-negotiable rights and safety limits from benefits that can be bargained over. Do not imply that compensation makes an unacceptable use acceptable.

Evidence, scenarios, and explicit decision triggersA way to act under uncertainty without choosing a prophecyRigorous and updateable; gives the tracker a continuing purposeCan feel technocratic; indicators may miss unmeasured harmsUse release-analysis skills to connect new evidence to durable demands

Specify what we know, what remains uncertain, and what findings would alter a decision. Pair monitoring with outcomes we want regardless of the forecast. A new data release should update an argument or intervention, not just add another chart.

The opening for ESP

Our inference: The promising contribution is a usable account of public authority, better working lives, and guaranteed economic security. Vanderbilt’s October 13 AI Governance Project announcement already integrates safety, public capacity, economic resilience, and democracy; Urban–AEI explicitly proposes evidence-led workforce policy under uncertainty. Integration and uncertainty alone are therefore insufficient claims to originality. ESP needs to demonstrate a distinctive contribution through the outcomes it prioritizes and the cases, tools, and campaigns it develops.

Pair an affirmative demand with an actual conflict, an institutional decision, and a result we can measure. Follow one case across technology, production, and distribution. That would let a reader explain why the same capability can lead to different outcomes and identify where to intervene.

ESP’s proposed advantage comes from connecting its antimonopoly, public-option, cash, and economic-security work. That is a strategic hypothesis to test with the team, not evidence of a unique or uncontested niche. Its AI transition brief provides an existing basis for that connection.

For the team discussion: Which pitch best matches the audience’s next decision? Which can we substantiate with one excellent case? Which would make someone return when the evidence changes?

# The Landscape's switch row holds the controls of the Links and Compare views, the menus first and the pills after them, and Copy Draft and Print act on what is on screen

**Status:** accepted (2026-11-24; the owner, in turn: "Start from should be in line with the views", "make this inline with the views" with Compare's bar pasted, 'Add "Add", "Copy Draft", and "Print" buttons', then "remove the add button and make sure the rest of the buttons are in line with the row across compare and link views", and finally "the policy selections should be before" Copy Draft, Copy link, Print, Randomize and Refresh).

**Context.** The Landscape has a switch of three buttons (Links, Compare, All). Compare's two menus and its pills sat in a bar of their own under it, and the Links view's menu in the view under it. The owner wanted them in line with the views. Each view also gained pills over the day (Back, Copy link, Randomize, Refresh), and she asked for Copy Draft and Print, and for an Add button, which was built (it put the policy on screen into a draft kept in the page, with a count) and taken out again the same day.

**Decision.**

- The controls of the view showing are on the switch's row (`.pol-subview-bar`), in a slot of their own beside the switch: `#polLinksSlot` and `#polCmpSlot`, each the shared filter bar's block (`.controls`, for its menu, label and pill styles) with the bar look turned off. Only the slot of the view showing is shown; All has none.
- In a slot the **menus** come first, then the six **pills**, each a unit (`.pol-subview-pick`, `.pol-subview-pills`). From about 1125px up the whole row is one line: the switch, the menus, the pills. The pills keep their size and end the row; the menus take what they leave, and the basis that decides when the pills no longer fit beside them (320px) is the same for Links' one menu and Compare's two, so on one line the pills end the row at the same place on both views. Where it does not fit, the pills drop together to a line of their own under the menus (about 1125px down to 780px), left-aligned, again the same on both views. Under about 780px the slot itself goes under the switch (the switch, the menus, then the pills), and on a phone each menu has a line of its own and the pills wrap in two lines. The order inside the row is the markup's order, so the keyboard meets what is seen. The pills are in alphabetical order, the owner's rule: Back, Copy Draft, Copy link, Print, Randomize, Refresh.
- **Copy Draft** copies the policy on screen (Links: the one in the middle; Compare: both, A then B, once when both menus say the same policy) as the dialog's own "Copy as draft" writes it, a rule between two (`policyToMarkdown`, `src/js/20-cite-export.js`). **Print** fills a sheet (`#polDraftSheet`, hidden on screen) with each policy on screen from a page of its own, in the dialog's words and classes (`injectPolicyLinks`), and opens the browser's print dialog; for printing the rest of the page is hidden, but only while no dialog is open (`:not(.pol-modal-open)`), so the dialog's own print rules keep working. `afterprint` puts everything back. Nothing is kept between presses: there is no list, no count and nothing that can be empty, so both buttons are always on.
- Compare's row stays under the top of the window while its long rows are read, as its two menus did when they were a bar of their own: `.is-pinned` on the bar (set while Compare shows) makes it sticky above 780px, where the row is one or two lines (about 57px or 91px); below that it scrolls away. The script keeps the bar's live height in `--pol-bar-h` for the ring's scroll margin, so a click on a sample brings the new pair to just under it.

**Consequences.**

- The order was changed twice in one afternoon, and both times it was the owner's call. Menus first was how the row was first built (her pasted Compare bar read that way). When she asked for "the rest of the buttons ... in line with the row across compare and link views" it was reversed, the pills straight after the switch and the menus after them, because that keeps the pills level with the switch and in one place at every width (menus first puts them on a second line below about 1125px). She then asked for "the policy selections" to be "before" the buttons, so it is menus first again, with the pills ending the row so that they are still in the same place on both views. The cost is the one that made the reversal: below about 1125px the pills are on a second line, under the menus, and the menus are narrow on one line (about 155px each for Compare's two at 1240px, enough for the start of a long title; the title is in full in the ring and the cards under them).
- The Compare view's bar is gone from the view (`.pol-cmp-bar`, `.pol-cmp-pills`), so its styles and tests were rewritten for the slot.
- The Add button and the shared draft were removed with their tests. If a draft that gathers policies across the two views is wanted later, it is a state and a count to add back, not a layout to redo; the version that existed for a few hours is described in the header comment of `src/js/32-policy-draft.js`, and nothing of it was ever committed.
- A page printed with the dialog open prints the dialog, as before; a page printed from a view prints that view's one or two policies. The print layout was checked in Chromium by showing the sheet with the print rules applied to the screen; it was not run through a printer or a PDF.
